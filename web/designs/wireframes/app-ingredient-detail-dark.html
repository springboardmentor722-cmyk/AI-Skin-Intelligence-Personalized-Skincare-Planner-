What it does

Glycolic acid is a gold-standard exfoliant. As the smallest molecule in the AHA family, it penetrates deeply into the skin to dissolve the "glue" that binds dead skin cells together. This process promotes cellular turnover, revealing a smoother, brighter complexion and improving overall skin texture.
