# 🌿 AI Skin Intelligence

AI Skin Intelligence is an AI-powered web application that provides personalized skincare recommendations by analyzing users' skin images and skin profiles. The platform combines Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and dermatologist guidance to help users understand their skin health and build an effective skincare routine.

---

## 📖 Project Overview

AI Skin Intelligence is designed to make skincare more intelligent and personalized. Users can upload their skin images, complete their skin profile, and receive AI-generated skin analysis along with product recommendations, ingredient information, and customized skincare routines.

The platform also supports consultants, dermatologists, and administrators through dedicated dashboards for better patient management and monitoring.

# 🔄 Application Workflow

1. User registers and logs into the application.
2. User completes the skin profile.
3. User uploads a skin image.
4. AI analyzes the uploaded image.
5. The system identifies skin type and skin concerns.
6. Personalized skincare products and ingredients are recommended.
7. A customized morning and night skincare routine is generated.
8. Users can monitor their skin improvement through progress tracking.
9. Users can book appointments with consultants or dermatologists if needed.
